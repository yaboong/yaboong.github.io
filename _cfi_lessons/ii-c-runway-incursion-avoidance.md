The Task C deck is a title slide only; this lesson is taught from the [Runway Incursion Avoidance]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-runway-incursion-avoidance/' | relative_url }}) module deck.

### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student understands what a runway incursion is and the three ways to prevent it: airport familiarity, situational awareness, and clear communication.
- The student can plan and brief a taxi, use airport diagrams and hot spot information, and follow safe procedures for entering, crossing and exiting runways.

### 2. Content

- Definition of a runway incursion and why it matters
- AC 91-73B single-pilot taxi procedures: taxi planning, NOTAMs and ATIS, current airport diagram and hot spots
- Finding the airport diagram (APD) and hot spot (HOT) pages and the Chart Supplement; KBAF hot spot example with satellite photos
- Pre-taxi briefing items, minimizing heads-down workload, written taxi instructions
- Airport markings, signs and lighting that protect the runway (linked from the Airport Markings & Signs and Airport Lights modules)
- Before entering a runway: explicit clearance, hold short lines, ILS critical areas, CTAF calls at non-towered airports
- Aircraft lighting for taxi, runway entry and takeoff; line up and wait (LUAW)
- After landing: clearing the runway completely, after-landing sequence
- Runway incursion prevention summary

### 3. Schedule (about 40 min)


| Segment | Time |
|---|---|
| Introduction (definition, three prevention areas) | 5 min |
| Taxi planning and airport information (KBAF hot spots) | 10 min |
| Briefing, workload, written instructions, markings and signs | 10 min |
| Runway entry, crossing, LUAW, lights, after landing | 10 min |
| Summary and review | 5 min |


### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the [Runway Incursion Avoidance]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-runway-incursion-avoidance/' | relative_url }}) module deck
- Current KBAF airport diagram and hot spot page (Terminal Procedures), Chart Supplement
- Whiteboard and markers for drawing taxi routes

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Define a runway incursion and introduce the three prevention areas.
- Show how to find the current airport diagram, hot spot page and Chart Supplement entry, and check effective dates.
- Walk through the KBAF hot spots on the diagram and satellite photos.
- Give the student a sample taxi clearance to write down, read back and trace on the diagram.
- Explain runway entry, crossing, LUAW and after-landing procedures, including aircraft lighting.
- Review with questions on markings, signs and hold short points.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Locate the airport diagram, hot spots and Chart Supplement information for a given airport.
- Brief a planned taxi route, including hold short points and runway crossings.
- Copy and read back a taxi clearance and trace it on the diagram.
- Explain the correct procedure and lighting for entering, crossing and exiting a runway.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student defines a runway incursion and explains the three prevention areas.
- The student plans and briefs a taxi route at KBAF, correctly identifying every hot spot and hold short point.
- The student states the procedures for runway entry, crossing, LUAW and after landing in line with AC 91-73B.

### 8. References

- AC 91-73 (Parts 91 and 135 Single Pilot, Flight School Procedures During Taxi Operations)
- AIM Ch. 2, Section 3 (Airport Marking Aids and Signs); Ch. 4, Section 3 (Airport Operations)
- Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25), Ch. 14 Airport Operations
- 14 CFR 91.129 (operations in Class D airspace: ATC clearance requirements on the surface)
- FAA Terminal Procedures (airport diagram and hot spot pages); Chart Supplement
