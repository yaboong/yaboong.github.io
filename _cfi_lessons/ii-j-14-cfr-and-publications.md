### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can find and read the regulations a pilot uses (14 CFR Parts 1, 61 and 91 and 49 CFR Part 830) and knows what each part covers.
- The student knows the purpose of FAA guidance and reference publications (ACs, SAFOs, InFOs, ACS/PTS, POH/AFM, AIM) and how to keep them current.

### 2. Content

- Structure of the CFR (title, chapter, subchapter, part, section) and the subchapters pilots use most
- Part 1: definitions (1.1), abbreviations (1.2), rules of construction (1.3: shall, may, must)
- Part 61 (certification) and Part 91 (general operating and flight rules) organization and applicability
- 49 CFR Part 830 (NTSB): definitions of accident, serious injury, substantial damage and incident; immediate notification (830.5) and its information (830.6); preservation of wreckage (830.10); reports (830.15)
- Non-regulatory guidance: Advisory Circulars, SAFOs and InFOs; ACs a CFI uses often
- ACS and PTS: structure and which tests use each (61.14 and Part 61 references)
- POH and AFM: who produces them, FAA-approved sections, required documents, GAMA section layout; 91.9 operating limitations
- AIM organization
- Currency of charts and publications (sectional 56-day cycle, Chart Supplement, other publications); 91.103 preflight action

### 3. Schedule (about 1 hour 30 min)


| Segment | Time |
|---|---|
| CFR structure, Parts 1, 61, 91 | 20 min |
| 49 CFR Part 830 | 25 min |
| ACs, SAFOs, InFOs, ACS and PTS | 15 min |
| POH/AFM and 91.9 | 10 min |
| AIM, publication currency, 91.103 | 15 min |
| Review scenarios | 5 min |


### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck
- eCFR website (14 CFR and 49 CFR) and the FAA AC, SAFO and InFO search pages
- Current FAR/AIM, PA-28-161 POH, ACS for the certificate being trained
- Current sectional chart and Chart Supplement
- Whiteboard and markers

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Show the CFR hierarchy on eCFR and have the student navigate to a given section.
- Read the exact text of key sections (1.3, 830.2, 830.5, 91.9, 91.103) and explain the wording.
- Give accident and incident scenarios and ask whether notification is required and when.
- Explain the difference between regulatory and non-regulatory material.
- Compare a POH and an AFM title page and walk through the POH sections.
- Show how to check that each chart and publication is current.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Find specific sections in 14 CFR and 49 CFR without help.
- Decide for each scenario whether it is an accident or incident, whether NTSB notification is required, and what report is due.
- Find a relevant AC, SAFO or InFO for a topic.
- Locate operating limitations and required information in the POH.
- Check the effective dates of their charts and publications.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student explains the purpose and content of 14 CFR Parts 1, 61 and 91 and 49 CFR Part 830 and finds requested sections quickly.
- The student correctly applies the Part 830 definitions and notification and reporting requirements to scenarios.
- The student explains the role of ACs, SAFOs, InFOs, ACS/PTS, the POH/AFM and the AIM, and verifies that publications are current.

### 8. References

- 14 CFR Part 1 (1.1 definitions, 1.2 abbreviations, 1.3 rules of construction); Part 61 (61.14 and subparts A through K); Part 91 (91.1 applicability, 91.9 civil aircraft flight manual and marking requirements, 91.103 preflight action)
- 49 CFR Part 830 (830.1, 830.2, 830.5, 830.6, 830.10, 830.15)
- Aeronautical Information Manual (chapter organization; 9-1-5 chart revision cycles)
- AC 00-2 (Advisory Circular Checklist and status); AC 61-65 (certification of pilots and flight and ground instructors) and other ACs listed in the deck
- FAA SAFO and InFO pages
- Airman Certification Standards for the certificate or rating being trained
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook (title page, Sections 1 through 9); GAMA Specification No. 1
- Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25), Ch. 1 Introduction to Flying (FAA regulations and publications)
