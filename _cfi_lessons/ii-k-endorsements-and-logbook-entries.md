### 1. Lesson Objective

- The learner understands the logbook entries and endorsements a flight instructor gives, from a student's first lesson through the practical test.
- The learner can prepare them with the correct regulation, date, make and model, and limitations, and knows the records and recent experience a flight instructor must keep.

### 2. Content

- Logbook entries for training given
  - required entries (§ 61.51(a), (b), (h))
  - instructor signature block: signature, date, certificate number, recent experience end date (§ 61.51(h)(2)(ii))
  - instructor must sign the logbook of each person trained (§ 61.189(a))
- Before training
  - U.S. citizenship or nationality verification, TSA eligibility for non-citizens (49 CFR §§ 1552.7, 1552.15), A.14
  - student pilot certificate through IACRA (§§ 61.83, 61.85, 61.193(b))
  - medical requirements (§ 61.23)
- Student pilot endorsements
  - pre-solo knowledge test and flight training (§ 61.87(b), (c)), A.3, A.4
  - solo, 90 days (§ 61.87(n), (p)), A.6, A.7
  - solo at night (§ 61.87(o)), A.5
  - solo to another airport within 25 NM (§ 61.93(b)(1)), A.8
  - solo cross-country (§ 61.93(c), (d)), A.9, A.10
  - repeated cross-country within 50 NM (§ 61.93(b)(2)), A.11
  - Class B airspace and airports (§ 61.95), A.12, A.13
- Other pilot endorsements
  - complex, high-performance, pressurized, tailwheel (§ 61.31(e)–(i)), A.72–A.75
  - solo without the category or class rating (§ 61.31(d)(2)), A.76
  - Special Federal Aviation Regulation example (SFAR 73)
- Practical test recommendation
  - knowledge test endorsements (§ 61.35(a)(1)), A.36, A.38
  - prerequisites (§ 61.39(a)), A.1, A.2
  - practical test endorsements: private A.37, commercial A.39, flight instructor A.45–A.49
  - additional aircraft ratings (§ 61.63), A.78
  - retest after failure (§ 61.49), A.77
- Flight review endorsement (§ 61.56), A.69
- Flight instructor records (§ 61.189)
- Flight instructor certificate duration, recent experience, and reinstatement (§§ 61.19(d), 61.197, 61.199)
- Risk: endorsements without limitations or expiration dates

### 3. Schedule (about 70 min)

| Segment | Time |
|---|---|
| Ground lesson | 45 min |
| Endorsement writing exercise | 15 min |
| Questions and review | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Title 14 CFR part 61 and 49 CFR part 1552 (current edition)
- AC 61-65K, Certification: Pilots and Flight and Ground Instructors
- Sample pilot logbook
- FAA Form 8710-1 / IACRA screens
- Endorsements and Logbook Entries slide deck
- Whiteboard and markers

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Present the ground lesson in the order of a student's training: before training, pre-solo, solo, cross-country, practical test.
- For each endorsement, state the regulation, who may give it, how long it is valid, and the AC 61-65K sample number.
- Show the required instructor signature block and flight instructor records.
- Explain flight instructor recent experience and reinstatement.
- Discuss the risk of endorsements missing limitations or expiration dates.
- Assign sample endorsements, review them, correct errors, and summarize.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Review AC 61-65K Appendix A before the lesson.
- Participate in discussion and ask questions.
- Write sample endorsements in a logbook: first solo (A.6), solo cross-country (A.9, A.10), private pilot practical test (A.1, A.37), flight review (A.69).
- Include the date, make and model, limitations, and the complete signature block in each one.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The learner can explain:
  - the required logbook entries for instruction given
  - student pilot pre-solo, solo, cross-country, and Class B endorsements
  - other required pilot endorsements, including an SFAR example
- The learner can prepare a practical test recommendation, with the correct endorsements and IACRA / FAA Form 8710-1 application, for initial certification, additional certification, and an additional aircraft rating.
- The learner can explain the flight review endorsement, required flight instructor records, and flight instructor renewal and reinstatement requirements.
- The learner can identify and mitigate the risk of endorsements without appropriate limitations or expiration dates.
- The learner can correctly prepare logbook entries or endorsements for at least two events.
