### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can perform steep turns (private about 45°, commercial about 50°) with coordinated control, holding altitude and airspeed, and explain the aerodynamics behind them.

### 2. Content

- What a steep turn is and what it develops; turn classes and aileron pressure
- Private vs commercial standards and sight pictures
- Aerodynamics: vertical lift loss, more AOA, more induced drag, airspeed loss; add power early
- Load factor (1/cos of bank) and stall speed increase; what it feels like
- Turn rate and radius vs airspeed and bank
- Control challenges: overbanking tendency, [left-turning tendencies]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-left-turning-tendencies/' | relative_url }}), adverse yaw, [slip vs skid]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-slip-and-skid/' | relative_url }}), left vs right turn rudder needs
- [Maneuvering speed]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-maneuvering-speed/' | relative_url }}) and personal Va
- Procedure: CRAAC setup, entry, in the turn, rollout (left and right)
- AFH common errors and fixes; video
- Risks: collision hazards, [low-altitude stall/spin and CFIT]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-low-altitude-maneuvering-cfit/' | relative_url }}), distraction

### 3. Schedule (ground about 50 min; flight about 45 min)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Definition, standards, aerodynamics, load factor | 20 min |
| Control challenges and maneuvering speed | 10 min |
| Procedure, common errors, video | 15 min |
| Risks | 5 min |
| Flight: demonstration and practice | 45 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck (video)
- PA-28-161 POH (maneuvering speed, stall speeds) and school checklist
- PA-28-161; model airplane

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Show the aerodynamic chain and load factor table so the student can predict what the airplane will do.
- Brief CRAAC and the entry/rollout sight pictures.
- Demonstrate both directions, then coach power, back pressure, rudder and rollout lead.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Explain load factor, stall speed increase and overbanking tendency.
- Clear the area and set up at or below Va.
- Fly 360° turns (commercial: then a turn in the opposite direction) holding altitude, airspeed and bank, rolling out on heading.

### 7. Completion Standards

- Private: 45° ±5° bank, altitude ±100 ft, airspeed ±10 kt, rollout heading ±10°. Commercial: 50° ±5°, same altitude and airspeed limits, rollout ±10°.
- The student keeps the turn coordinated and maintains outside scan for traffic.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 10 Performance Maneuvers (steep turns, common errors); Ch. 3 (turns)
- Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25), Ch. 5 Aerodynamics of Flight (load factor, Va, left-turning tendencies)
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 2 (maneuvering speed), Section 5 (stall speed chart)
- Private and Commercial Pilot Airman Certification Standards (steep turns)
