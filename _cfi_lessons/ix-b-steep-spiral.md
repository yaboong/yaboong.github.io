### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can perform a steep spiral — a power-idle gliding turn of constant radius around a ground point — holding airspeed and correcting bank for wind, and relate it to an engine-failure descent over a landing site.

### 2. Content

- What a steep spiral is: at least three 360° turns at idle, constant radius, bank 60° or less, completed no lower than 1,500 ft AGL; use in an engine failure
- AFH figure; bank follows groundspeed (G5 groundspeed)
- Choosing the numbers: stall speed by bank, why 80 KIAS, starting bank
- Constant radius in wind: steepest bank downwind, shallowest upwind; altitude used per turn; wind changes with altitude
- Holding 80 KIAS as bank changes
- Setup (CRAAC) and procedure: entry downwind over the point, spiral, engine clearing each 360°, counting turns, rollout
- Video; risks and hard limits; AFH common errors

### 3. Schedule (ground about 40 min; flight about 40 min)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Definition, numbers and wind | 15 min |
| Airspeed control, setup and procedure | 15 min |
| Video, risks, common errors | 10 min |
| Flight: demonstration and practice | 40 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck (video)
- PA-28-161 POH (stall speeds, best glide) and school checklist
- PA-28-161 (G5 groundspeed); a clear ground reference point

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Explain why the bank must change with groundspeed and how airspeed is held with pitch.
- Brief the entry, engine clearing and rollout; set the altitude floor.
- Demonstrate, then coach bank changes and airspeed control.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Choose a point and enter downwind over it at the briefed airspeed.
- Hold a constant radius with bank changes and airspeed with pitch, clearing the engine each turn.
- Roll out on the briefed heading above the altitude floor.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student completes at least three 360° turns with constant radius, bank 60° or less, airspeed ±10 kt, rollout heading ±10°, and recovers no lower than 1,500 ft AGL.
