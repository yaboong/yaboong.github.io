### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can perform lazy eights with constantly changing pitch, bank and airspeed, showing coordination and planning over a wide range of airspeeds and attitudes.

### 2. Content

- Definition: two opposite 180° turns with climbing and descending 90° segments; purpose
- AFH figure and attitudes at the 45°, 90°, 135° and 180° points
- Profile targets: pitch, airspeed and altitude at each point
- Setup (CRAAC): cardinal heading, references, entry speed at or below Va, altitude floor
- Flying it: pitch/bank/airspeed/altitude table; coordination differences for right and left turns; instructor tips (rudder, heading bug)
- Standards, risks, common errors

### 3. Schedule (ground about 40 min; flight about 40 min)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Definition, AFH figure, profile | 10 min |
| Setup and flying it, coordination | 15 min |
| Standards, risks, common errors | 15 min |
| Flight: demonstration and practice | 40 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck
- PA-28-161 POH (maneuvering speed, stall speeds) and school checklist
- PA-28-161 (heading bug / HSI course); model airplane

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Draw the maneuver against the horizon references and walk the attitude at each point.
- Explain why rudder needs differ between right and left turns.
- Demonstrate, then coach smooth, constant control changes.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Choose references for the 45°, 90°, 135° and 180° points.
- Fly symmetrical loops with the correct pitch and bank at each point.
- Return to entry altitude and airspeed at the 180° points.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student keeps control changes constant, reaches about 30° bank at the 90° points, and at each 180° point is within ±100 ft of entry altitude, ±10 kt of entry airspeed and ±10° of heading, no lower than 1,500 ft AGL.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 10 Performance Maneuvers (lazy eights, common errors)
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 2 (maneuvering speed)
- Commercial Pilot Airman Certification Standards (lazy eights)
