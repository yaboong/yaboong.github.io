### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can fly a rectangular course, S-turns and turns around a point, correcting for wind drift while dividing attention between the ground track, airplane control and traffic.

### 2. Content

- Purpose: track over the ground with wind correction, divided attention, basis for the traffic pattern
- Assessing the wind; constant-bank turn drifting downwind (AFH figure); G5 PFD/HSI reference
- Wind correction angle and the G5 ground track indicator
- Constant radius in wind: bank follows groundspeed (worked example), why enter downwind, bank limit
- Before the flight: winds aloft, practice area, entry plan; setup CRAAC (altitude 600–1,000 ft AGL, [Va]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-maneuvering-speed/' | relative_url }}))
- Turns around a point: procedure, diagrams, AFH figure, video
- S-turns: procedure by side, AFH figure, diagram
- Rectangular course vs traffic pattern; AFH figure
- Common errors; risks ([low altitude/CFIT]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-low-altitude-maneuvering-cfit/' | relative_url }}), collision hazards, distraction)

### 3. Schedule (ground about 50 min; flight about 45 min)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose, wind assessment, wind correction | 15 min |
| Constant radius and groundspeed | 10 min |
| Planning and setup | 5 min |
| Turns around a point, S-turns, rectangular course | 15 min |
| Common errors and risks | 5 min |
| Flight: demonstration and practice | 45 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck (video)
- Winds aloft (Aviation Weather Center), sectional chart for the practice area
- PA-28-161 (G5 PFD/HSI ground track); model airplane and whiteboard

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Draw each maneuver with the wind and mark where bank is steepest and shallowest.
- Relate the rectangular course to the traffic pattern.
- Demonstrate each maneuver; coach the student to keep eyes outside and correct bank with groundspeed.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Determine the wind and plan a downwind entry.
- Fly each maneuver holding a uniform ground track with the correct bank and crab.
- Hold altitude and airspeed while scanning for traffic and obstacles.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student flies each maneuver at 600–1,000 ft AGL, holding altitude ±100 ft and airspeed ±10 kt, bank not exceeding 45°, with a uniform ground track and correct wind correction.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 7 Ground Reference Maneuvers (rectangular course, S-turns, turns around a point, common errors); Ch. 8 (traffic pattern relationship)
- 14 CFR 91.119 (minimum safe altitudes)
- Garmin G5 Pilot's Guide (ground track, groundspeed)
- Private Pilot Airman Certification Standards (ground reference maneuvers)
