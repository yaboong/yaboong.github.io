### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can decide whether the pilot, the airplane and the environment are ready for the flight.
- The student can preflight the PA-28-161 by checklist, explaining what to check, why, and how to spot defects.

### 2. Content

- Pilot self-assessment: experience, currency, IMSAFE, PAVE ([Personal Minimums]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-personal-minimums/' | relative_url }}) module)
- Airplane appropriate (equipment, runways, load, fuel) and airworthy (3.5(a), 91.7(b)); inspections and documents ([Airworthiness Requirements]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/iii-b-airworthiness-requirements/' | relative_url }}))
- How to inspect: checklist sequence, distortion, loose rivets, cracks; anything out of place → mechanic, the PIC decides
- Cabin: Hobbs and tach, ignition off, master on for fuel gauges, stall warning and lights, flaps
- Fuel: visual quantity, caps, sumps, grade and color, water and sediment, vents
- Wings, landing gear, engine and propeller, fuselage and tail by the school checklist
- Environment: weather (91.103), route and terrain, airports and NOTAMs, airspace, night
- Security: citizenship or TSA eligibility before training, suspicious activity, securing the airplane, reporting

### 3. Schedule (about 1 hour 30 min)

| Segment | Time |
|---|---|
| Pilot self-assessment, appropriate and airworthy | 20 min |
| Preflight inspection walk-around | 45 min |
| Environmental factors | 10 min |
| Aviation security and review | 15 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck
- PA-28-161 POH and the school preflight checklist
- N79NA and a fuel tester
- Weather briefing, NOTAMs, sectional chart

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Run IMSAFE and PAVE for today's flight with the student.
- Demonstrate the walk-around by checklist, explaining the reason for each item.
- Show real defect examples and ask for a go/no-go decision.
- Review the environment for the route and the security procedures.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Complete the self-assessment and determine that the airplane is appropriate and airworthy.
- Preflight the airplane by checklist and explain each item.
- Sump the fuel and identify grade, water and sediment.
- Brief the environmental and security items.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student completes the preflight by checklist and explains what, why and how for each item, with the related regulations.
- The student makes a correct go/no-go decision for the pilot, airplane and environment.
- The student identifies external pressures and security concerns and how to manage them.
