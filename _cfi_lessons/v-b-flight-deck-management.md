### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can brief passengers, use checklists correctly and secure the cabin before flight.
- The student can manage automation, inoperative equipment and passenger distractions using ADM and SRM.

### 2. Content

- Passenger briefing (91.107(a)): belts and harnesses, when to fasten, approved seats; PIC, propeller avoidance
- SAFETY briefing and positive exchange of flight controls
- Checklists: purpose, running them early, complacency, interruptions, emergency memory items
- Navigation data: current database required for IFR, recommended for VFR; 28-day cycle; checking GTN and ForeFlight
- Securing items: nothing near the rudder pedals, baggage secured, seat locked
- Automation and portable electronic devices: over-reliance, ADS-B In limits, iPad backups
- Inoperative equipment: 91.213(d) decision path, placarding; lights, instruments and radios (91.205)
- Inoperative transponder: where Mode C and ADS-B Out are required (91.215, 91.225)
- Passenger distractions, the passenger as a resource, SRM 5 Ps

### 3. Schedule (about 1 hour 15 min)

| Segment | Time |
|---|---|
| Passenger briefing, exchange of flight controls | 15 min |
| Checklists and navigation data | 15 min |
| Securing items, automation, portable electronic devices | 10 min |
| Inoperative equipment and transponder scenarios | 25 min |
| Passenger distractions, SRM, review | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck
- School PA-28-161 checklist and POH
- GTN trainer app, iPad with ForeFlight
- Sectional chart for local airports

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Demonstrate a complete passenger briefing and the exchange of flight controls.
- Show proper checklist use and the common traps.
- Show database currency on the GTN and in ForeFlight.
- Give inoperative-equipment and transponder scenarios and ask for a go/no-go decision with the regulation.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Give a complete passenger briefing.
- Use the checklist correctly and secure all items.
- Verify navigation data currency.
- Apply 91.213(d) and 91.205 to inoperative-equipment scenarios.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student gives a passenger briefing that covers 91.107 and the ACS items.
- The student uses checklists appropriately and verifies current navigation data.
- The student correctly decides whether flight is permitted with inoperative equipment, including the transponder.
