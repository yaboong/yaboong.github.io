### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can start the PA-28-161 engine by checklist under cold, hot, flooded and external power conditions.
- The student knows the starting limitations and propeller safety, and positions the airplane safely before start.

### 2. Content

- Before start and engine start checklists, and the reason for each step
- Cold, hot, flooded starts from the POH; cold-weather preheat
- Starting limitations: starter cranking and cool-down, oil pressure within 30 seconds, warm-up
- Engine fire on start: cause and checklist order
- External power: POH procedure, polarity, cable removal risks
- Propeller safety: clear area, wind, surface, brakes or chocks, "CLEAR"
- Turning the propeller by hand: always assume the ignition is on
- Hand propping: school policy, procedure and calls

### 3. Schedule (about 1 hour)

| Segment | Time |
|---|---|
| Checklists and the reason for each step | 15 min |
| Cold, hot, flooded starts and limitations | 15 min |
| Engine fire on start, external power | 10 min |
| Propeller safety, hand propping, review | 20 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck (videos)
- PA-28-161 POH (Section 3, Section 4) and the school checklist
- N79NA for a start demonstration

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Explain each checklist step, then demonstrate a start in the airplane.
- Compare cold, hot and flooded starts using the POH.
- Explain the starting limitations and the engine fire on start actions.
- Stress propeller safety and positioning before start.

### 6. Student's Actions

- State the checklist items and the reason for each.
- Describe the starts under various conditions and with external power.
- State the starting limitations and engine fire actions.
- Position the airplane and start the engine by checklist.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student starts the engine by checklist and correctly checks the indications after start.
- The student states the starting limitations and the actions when they are exceeded.
- The student positions the airplane safely and follows propeller safety procedures.

### 8. References

- PA-28-161 POH: Section 3 (engine fire during start), Section 4 (starting engine, warm-up); school checklist
- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 2 Ground Operations
- Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25), Ch. 7 Aircraft Systems
- FAA-S-ACS-25, Area of Operation V, Task C
