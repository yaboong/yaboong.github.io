### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can plan and conduct a safe taxi at towered and non-towered airports, including clearances, readbacks and self-announcements.
- The student can identify airport markings, signs and lighting, correct for wind while taxiing, and recognize and avoid runway incursion risks.

### 2. Content

- Example flight: day VFR from Westfield-Barnes (KBAF, towered) to Orange (KORE); taxi at KBAF as the running example
- Current airport information: airport diagram, hot spots, Chart Supplement, NOTAMs; KBAF hot spots HS1 and HS2
- Pre-taxi checklist and briefing (brake check, controls for wind, PIC responsibilities, positive exchange of controls, taxi route, readbacks, sterile cockpit); setting up avionics before moving
- Planning the taxi from ATIS (runways in use, intersection departure) on the satellite photo and airport diagram
- Movement vs non-movement area and the boundary marking; Ground clearance before entering the movement area
- Taxi clearances and readbacks (hold short readback, expectation bias example); non-towered CTAF self-announce at Northampton (7B2)
- Safe taxiing technique: lights, clearing before turns, walking pace, power before brakes, following the centerline, sterile cockpit
- Taxi risks: parallel active runways, runway change while taxiing, low visibility and progressive taxi, distractions
- Taxiway markings at the ramp exit (boundary, edge, centerline); continuous vs dashed edge markings
- Wind direction indicators and taxi control positions (quartering headwind, quartering tailwind) practiced on the KBAF ramp
- Summaries (modules): [Runway Incursion Avoidance]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-runway-incursion-avoidance/' | relative_url }}), [Airport Markings & Signs]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-airport-markings-signs/' | relative_url }}), [Airport Lights]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-airport-lights/' | relative_url }})

### 3. Schedule (about 1 hour 30 min)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Airport information, hot spots, pre-taxi briefing | 15 min |
| Taxi planning at KBAF, movement area | 10 min |
| Clearances, readbacks, CTAF announcements | 15 min |
| Safe taxi technique and taxi risks | 15 min |
| Taxiway markings and wind correction practice | 20 min |
| Markings, signs and lighting review, runway incursion summary | 15 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck
- KBAF and 7B2 airport diagrams and hot spot pages (Terminal Procedures), Chart Supplement, current NOTAMs and ATIS
- iPad with ForeFlight (taxi route, airport diagram)
- Model airplane or yoke picture for wind-correction practice
- Whiteboard and markers

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Brief a real taxi from the KBAF ramp using the ATIS, airport diagram and hot spots.
- Role-play Ground clearances, including a hold short instruction and an unexpected runway assignment, and correct readbacks.
- Quiz the student on which line to follow at the ramp exit and on each sign and marking type.
- Give wind and heading combinations and have the student place the controls; repeat after each turn on the taxi route.
- Stress stopping the airplane before any heads-down task and asking for progressive taxi when unsure.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Find the hot spots and plan the taxi route before engine start.
- Give the pre-taxi briefing and complete the brake check.
- Make correct readbacks (towered) and self-announcements (non-towered).
- Identify markings, signs and lights and state the required action for each.
- Place the flight controls correctly for any wind direction while taxiing.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student plans and briefs a taxi route that accounts for hot spots and NOTAMs.
- The student reads back all taxi and hold short instructions correctly and never crosses a hold short line without clearance.
- The student correctly identifies all airport markings, signs and lighting covered.
- The student positions the flight controls correctly for the wind and taxis at a safe speed on the centerline.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 2 Ground Operations (taxiing, wind correction while taxiing)

- AC 91-73 (Parts 91 and 135 Single Pilot, Flight School Procedures During Taxi Operations)
- AC 150/5340-1 (Standards for Airport Markings); AC 150/5340-18 (Standards for Airport Sign Systems)
- Chart Supplement and Terminal Procedures (airport diagrams, hot spots) for KBAF and 7B2
- FAA runway safety resources (runway incursion avoidance)
