### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can complete the PA-28-161 engine run-up and before takeoff check by checklist, in a proper location, while keeping attention outside.
- The student can brief and plan for a rejected takeoff or engine failure after liftoff, using POH performance data.

### 2. Content

- Overview: engine run-up and before takeoff check
- PA-28-161 POH Section 4 Ground Check: magneto check at 2000 RPM (drop and difference limits), vacuum, annunciator test, carburetor heat check, electric fuel pump check, engine gauges
- PA-28-161 POH Section 4 Before Takeoff items
- School checklist flow and the departure briefing with emergency brief
- Where to run up: run-up areas at KBAF, 7B2 and 0B5; heading into the wind, prop wash away from others, firm surface free of debris; airports with no run-up pad
- Run-up video walk-through
- Rejected takeoff and engine failure after liftoff: abort point, accelerate-stop estimate from POH takeoff and landing charts, 50/70 rule of thumb
- Division of attention during the run-up; unexpected runway change after the run-up
- Wake turbulence as a takeoff risk ([Wake Turbulence]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-wake-turbulence/' | relative_url }}) module)

### 3. Schedule (about 1 hour)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Overview and POH ground check | 15 min |
| POH before takeoff items, checklist and departure briefing | 10 min |
| Run-up location and video | 10 min |
| Rejected takeoff, engine failure after liftoff, POH estimate | 15 min |
| Division of attention, runway change, wake turbulence, review | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck (run-up video)
- PA-28-161 POH (Section 4 Normal Procedures, Section 5 takeoff and landing charts) and the school checklist
- Airport diagrams for KBAF, 7B2, 0B5
- N79NA for a run-up demonstration
- Whiteboard and markers

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Explain the purpose and limits of each run-up item from the POH, not just the checklist line.
- Demonstrate a run-up in the airplane, keeping eyes outside and calling out each item.
- Have the student choose a run-up spot on each airport diagram and explain why.
- Work the accelerate-stop estimate from the POH charts and set an abort point with the student.
- Have the student give a full departure briefing, including the emergency brief.

### 6. Student's Actions

- State the run-up limits from the POH and what to do if a check fails.
- Perform the run-up and before takeoff check by checklist while monitoring traffic and the radio.
- Choose and position the airplane correctly in a run-up area.
- Compute the accelerate-stop estimate and choose an abort point.
- Give a complete departure and emergency briefing.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student completes the run-up and before takeoff check in the correct order and correctly interprets every indication against POH limits.
- The student positions the airplane correctly and maintains outside awareness throughout.
- The student gives a complete departure briefing with a planned abort point and engine failure actions.

### 8. References

- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 4 Normal Procedures (ground check, before takeoff, takeoff); Section 5 Performance (short field takeoff distance, landing ground roll)
- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 2 Ground Operations (before takeoff check); Ch. 6 Takeoffs and Departure Climbs (rejected takeoff, engine failure after liftoff)
- Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25), Ch. 7 Aircraft Systems (ignition, carburetor heat, vacuum system)
- Aeronautical Information Manual: Ch. 7, Wake Turbulence section; AC 90-23 (Aircraft Wake Turbulence)
- School PA-28-161 checklist
