### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can choose the correct runway, pattern direction and altitude, and enter, fly and depart the traffic pattern at towered and non-towered airports.
- The student can communicate in the pattern, apply right-of-way rules, maintain spacing and recognize pattern risks such as the base-to-final turn, wake turbulence and runway changes.

### 2. Content

- Pattern legs (AIM Figure 4-3-1) and the 2025 AIM change (upwind as an extension of departure, [Departure vs. Upwind Leg]({% post_url 2025-10-18-upwind-leg-departure-leg-AIM-updates %})); AC 90-66C as non-regulatory guidance
- Pattern selection: visual indicators, segmented circle, wind indicators, runway most aligned with the wind
- Direction: left traffic by default (91.126(b)); right traffic from the Chart Supplement and sectional (Pittsfield KPSF example)
- Altitude: 1,000 ft AGL for propeller airplanes, other categories; finding TPA in the Chart Supplement and ForeFlight
- Entry: 45-degree entry at TPA abeam midfield, entries from the upwind side, straight-in considerations, departing the pattern
- Collision avoidance: where midair collisions happen, see and avoid, lights, no-radio aircraft
- CTAF self-announce content and practices; example calls
- Right-of-way in the pattern (91.113)
- Pattern work: turn points, wind correction (rectangular course), short and low approach
- Towered patterns: ATC-directed entry, "report midfield", cleared for the option; Westfield-Barnes (KBAF) Chart Supplement data; tower phraseology and video
- Reference modules: [Light Signals]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-light-signals/' | relative_url }}), [Airport Markings & Signs]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-airport-markings-signs/' | relative_url }}), [Airport Lights]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-airport-lights/' | relative_url }})
- Risks: runway incursion, collision hazards, base-to-final turn stall/spin, [slip vs skid]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-slip-and-skid/' | relative_url }}), [wake turbulence]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-wake-turbulence/' | relative_url }}), [wind shear]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-wind-shear/' | relative_url }}), runway changes; local airports 7B2 and KPSF
- Common errors: altitude and airspeed, task saturation, lookout, radio

### 3. Schedule (about 2 hours)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Pattern legs, selection, direction, altitude | 25 min |
| Entries, departures, collision avoidance | 20 min |
| CTAF calls and right-of-way | 20 min |
| Pattern work, wind correction, towered patterns | 25 min |
| Risks: base-to-final, wake turbulence, wind shear, runway changes | 20 min |
| Common errors and review | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck (videos)
- Chart Supplement and sectional chart (KPSF, KBAF, 7B2), ForeFlight
- AC 90-66C and AIM 4-3
- Model airplane and whiteboard for drawing patterns and entries
- PA-28-161 POH (stall speed chart)

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Draw the pattern and have the student label each leg, turn point and altitude.
- Use KPSF to work out runway, direction and TPA from the Chart Supplement and sectional.
- Have the student plan an entry from each side of the airport, including from the upwind side.
- Role-play CTAF and tower calls through a full pattern.
- Explain the base-to-final stall/spin risk with the PA-28-161 stall speeds in a bank.
- Present runway-change and wake turbulence scenarios and ask for the safe action.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Determine runway, pattern direction and TPA for a given airport and wind.
- Plan and describe the correct entry and departure for each direction of arrival.
- Make all pattern calls at towered and non-towered airports.
- State right-of-way rules in the pattern.
- Explain wind correction on each leg and the actions to avoid a base-to-final stall/spin.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student correctly determines pattern direction, altitude and entry for towered and non-towered airports.
- The student makes correct, concise pattern calls and applies right-of-way rules.
- The student explains pattern risks and the actions to manage them, including go-around decisions.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 8 Airport Traffic Patterns; Ch. 7 Ground Reference Maneuvers (rectangular course)
- Aeronautical Information Manual: Ch. 4, Section 3 Airport Operations (traffic patterns, Figure 4-3-1, option approach); Ch. 7 wake turbulence section
- AC 90-66C (Non-Towered Airport Flight Operations)
- 14 CFR 91.113 (right-of-way rules), 91.117 (aircraft speed), 91.126 and 91.129 (operations in Class G and Class D airspace)
- Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25), Ch. 14 Airport Operations (traffic patterns, segmented circle, wind indicators)
- Chart Supplement entries (KPSF, KBAF, 7B2); PA-28-161 POH Section 5 (stall speed chart)
