### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can perform a normal takeoff and climb in the PA-28-161 with directional control on the centerline, rotation at the correct speed, and a stable Vy climb.
- The student understands left-turning tendencies, the effect of wind, and the common errors in the takeoff and climb.

### 2. Content

- Prerequisite: [Takeoff and Climb]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-takeoff-and-climb/' | relative_url }}) module
- What "normal" means (AFH): into the wind, firm surface, no obstructions, adequate runway
- [Left-turning tendencies]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-left-turning-tendencies/' | relative_url }})
- Takeoff roll: smooth full power, rudder for centerline, engine instrument check, control effectiveness as speed builds
- Lift-off and initial climb (AFH): back pressure, climb attitude, trim, Vy to 500 ft above terrain, then cruise climb
- PA-28-161 POH normal takeoff and climb speeds (rotate 55, Vy 79, Vx 63, cruise climb 87)
- School procedure callouts on the AFH takeoff figure
- AFH common errors
- [Crosswind takeoff]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-crosswind-takeoff/' | relative_url }})

### 3. Schedule (ground about 40 min; flight about 40 min)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Normal conditions, left-turning tendencies | 10 min |
| Takeoff roll, lift-off, initial climb | 15 min |
| POH speeds, school callouts, crosswind takeoff | 10 min |
| Common errors | 5 min |
| Flight: demonstration and practice | 40 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck
- PA-28-161 POH (Section 4 normal takeoff and climb, Section 5 takeoff chart) and school checklist
- N79NA

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Explain each left-turning tendency and when it is strongest during the takeoff.
- Brief the callouts and speeds, then demonstrate a takeoff with callouts.
- Coach rudder use, rotation, climb attitude and trim during practice.

### 6. Student's Actions

- State the POH speeds and the takeoff callouts.
- Fly the takeoff with smooth power, centerline tracking and rotation at the correct speed.
- Hold Vy to 500 ft above terrain, then transition to cruise climb.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student maintains the centerline, rotates at the POH speed, and climbs at Vy (+10/−5 kt private, ±5 kt commercial) with the correct configuration.
- The student compensates for left-turning tendencies and wind throughout.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 6 Takeoffs and Departure Climbs (normal takeoff, crosswind takeoff, common errors)
- Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25), Ch. 5 Aerodynamics of Flight (left-turning tendencies)
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 4 Normal Procedures (normal takeoff, climb); Section 5 Performance (takeoff distance)
- Private and Commercial Pilot Airman Certification Standards (normal takeoff and climb)
