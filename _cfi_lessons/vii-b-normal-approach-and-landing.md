### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can fly a stabilized normal approach and landing in the PA-28-161 with the correct configuration, airspeeds and touchdown on the centerline at the selected point.
- The student can judge the approach path visually, correct for wind and gusts, recognize an unstabilized approach and go around, and manage the risks of the approach and landing phase.

### 2. Content

- What "normal" means (AFH conditions); non-normal airports and runway data in the Chart Supplement
- [Personal minimums]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-personal-minimums/' | relative_url }}); factors affecting approach and landing (runway conditions, wind, density altitude, weight); [LAHSO]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-lahso/' | relative_url }})
- Landing performance: POH landing chart best vs worst case; density, true airspeed, ground speed
- Runway selection
- Landing phases; pattern procedure with PA-28-161 numbers (downwind, abeam, base, final speeds, power, flaps)
- Base-to-final turn risk: load factor and stall speed in a bank, [slip vs skid]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-slip-and-skid/' | relative_url }})
- Final approach: flaps 40, POH approach speeds, trim, centerline alignment
- Stabilized approach: aiming point vs touchdown point, runway shape on a stable path, height judgment, PAPI
- Energy management (pitch and power corrections)
- Round-out, flare, touchdown, roll-out and after-landing
- Wind correction in the pattern (7B2 examples); [crosswind landing]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-crosswind-landing/' | relative_url }}); gusty air (half the gust factor)
- AFH common errors; private vs commercial standards
- Stabilized approach criteria and go-around flow ([Go-Around/Rejected Landing]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/vii-n-go-around-rejected-landing/' | relative_url }}))
- Radio calls at non-towered and towered airports
- Risks: distraction, [wind shear]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-wind-shear/' | relative_url }}), [wake turbulence]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-wake-turbulence/' | relative_url }}), collision hazards and [runway incursion]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-runway-incursion-avoidance/' | relative_url }}), [low-altitude stall/spin and CFIT]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-low-altitude-maneuvering-cfit/' | relative_url }})

### 3. Schedule (ground about 1 hour 45 min; flight about 1 hour)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Normal conditions, factors, landing performance, runway selection | 25 min |
| Landing phases and pattern procedure with numbers | 20 min |
| Final approach, stabilized approach, height judgment, energy management | 25 min |
| Round-out to after landing, wind and gust corrections | 15 min |
| Common errors, standards, go-around, radio, risks | 20 min |
| Flight: demonstration, then pattern practice (several landings) | 60 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck (videos, simulator sequence)
- PA-28-161 POH (Section 4 approach and landing, Section 5 landing chart) and school checklist
- Chart Supplement (7B2, KBAF, local airports), ForeFlight
- N79NA; whiteboard and model airplane

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Brief the pattern with exact power, airspeed and flap settings for each point.
- Explain how to judge the path with the aiming point and runway shape, using the simulator sequence.
- Demonstrate a normal landing with callouts, then coach the student through practice landings.
- Set go-around criteria before flight and call for a go-around when the approach is not stabilized.
- Debrief each landing against the stabilized approach criteria and the standards.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Compute landing distance from the POH chart and select a runway.
- State the pattern numbers and stabilized approach criteria.
- Fly the pattern and approach with the correct configuration and airspeeds, correcting for wind.
- Round out, flare and touch down on the centerline near the selected point, and complete the after-landing items.
- Go around when the approach is not stabilized.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student consistently flies a stabilized approach at the POH approach speed (+10/−5 kt private, ±5 kt commercial).
- The student touches down smoothly on the centerline within the required distance of the selected point, with the longitudinal axis aligned.
- The student recognizes an unstabilized approach and executes a timely go-around.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 9 Approaches and Landings (normal approach and landing, stabilized approach, crosswind, turbulent air, go-around, common errors); Ch. 8 Airport Traffic Patterns
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 4 Normal Procedures (approach and landing); Section 5 Performance (landing distance)
- Aeronautical Information Manual: Ch. 2, Section 1 (VASI/PAPI); Ch. 4, Section 3 Airport Operations (LAHSO, traffic patterns); Ch. 7 wake turbulence section
- Chart Supplement entries for the airports used
- Private and Commercial Pilot Airman Certification Standards (normal approach and landing)
