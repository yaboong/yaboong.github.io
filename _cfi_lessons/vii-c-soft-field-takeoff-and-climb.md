### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can perform a soft-field takeoff and climb, transferring the weight from the wheels to the wings as soon as possible and using ground effect to accelerate safely to climb speed.

### 2. Content

- Prerequisite: [Takeoff and Climb]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-takeoff-and-climb/' | relative_url }}) module
- Soft fields (grass, dirt, snow) and why the technique is different
- AFH technique: keep moving, flaps, back pressure for a nose-high attitude, lift off below climb speed in ground effect
- Wingtip vortices and ground effect (PHAK figures); ground effect numbers for the PA-28-161 wingspan
- Climbing out of ground effect: induced drag rises, settling below Vx
- AFH figures: takeoff in ground effect vs premature lift-off
- Lift-off and initial climb: accelerate in ground effect, then climb at Vx (obstacle) or Vy
- Left-turning tendencies at high pitch (more right rudder)
- PA-28-161 POH soft-field takeoff (flaps 25°, 52 KIAS with obstacle, Vy 79) and school procedures (no obstacle, obstacle)
- AFH common errors; ACS skill standards

### 3. Schedule (ground about 45 min; flight about 40 min)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Soft fields and AFH technique | 10 min |
| Wingtip vortices and ground effect | 15 min |
| Lift-off, climb, left-turning tendencies | 5 min |
| POH and school procedures | 10 min |
| Common errors and standards | 5 min |
| Flight: demonstration and practice | 40 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck (video)
- PA-28-161 POH (Section 4 soft-field takeoff, Section 5 takeoff chart) and school checklist
- N79NA; a grass runway if available, otherwise simulated on a paved runway

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Explain ground effect with the PHAK figures and why the airplane can fly before it can climb.
- Brief the procedure without and with an obstacle.
- Demonstrate, then coach back pressure, right rudder and the level-off in ground effect.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Explain ground effect and the danger of climbing out of it too slowly.
- Taxi onto the runway without stopping and apply full power smoothly with back pressure.
- Lift off at the lowest speed, accelerate in ground effect, then climb at Vx or Vy with the correct flap retraction.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student keeps the airplane moving, lifts off at the lowest practical speed, and accelerates in ground effect before climbing.
- The student climbs at Vx or Vy (+10/−5 kt private, ±5 kt commercial) with directional control throughout.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 6 Takeoffs and Departure Climbs (soft/rough-field takeoff and climb, ground effect on takeoff, common errors)
- Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25), Ch. 5 Aerodynamics of Flight (wingtip vortices, ground effect, left-turning tendencies)
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 4 Normal Procedures (soft field takeoff, climb)
- Private and Commercial Pilot Airman Certification Standards (soft-field takeoff and climb)
