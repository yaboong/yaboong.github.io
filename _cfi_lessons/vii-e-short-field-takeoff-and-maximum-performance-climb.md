### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can perform a short-field takeoff and maximum performance climb in the PA-28-161 following the POH procedure, clearing an obstacle with the steepest climb.

### 2. Content

- Real short runways: Lukla, St. Barts, Saba (runway length, elevation, terrain and obstacles)
- AFH purpose and technique: all runway used, shortest ground roll, rotate near Vx, climb at Vx until clear, then Vy
- Effects of a premature lift-off (AFH figure)
- PA-28-161 POH short-field takeoff: obstacle (flaps 25) and no-obstacle procedures
- School procedure step by step (flaps, brakes, full power, rotate, Vx, flap retraction, Vy)
- AFH common errors; ACS skill standards

### 3. Schedule (ground about 40 min; flight about 45 min)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose and real-world examples | 10 min |
| AFH technique and premature lift-off | 10 min |
| POH and school procedure | 10 min |
| Common errors and standards | 10 min |
| Flight: demonstration and practice | 45 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck
- PA-28-161 POH (Section 4 short-field takeoff, Section 5 takeoff chart) and school checklist
- N79NA

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Explain why using all the runway and rotating at the right speed matter.
- Compute takeoff distance over a 50-ft obstacle for the day's conditions.
- Demonstrate the procedure with callouts, then coach practice takeoffs.

### 6. Student's Actions

- State the POH and school procedures and speeds.
- Compute the takeoff distance and decide whether the runway is adequate.
- Fly the procedure, holding Vx until clear of the obstacle, then Vy, retracting flaps at the right points.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student flies the short-field takeoff with the correct configuration, rotates at the correct speed, and holds Vx (+10/−5 kt private) until the obstacle is cleared, then Vy.
- The student explains the risks of premature lift-off and early flap retraction.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 6 Takeoffs and Departure Climbs (short-field takeoff and maximum performance climb, common errors)
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 4 Normal Procedures (short field takeoff); Section 5 Performance (takeoff distance)
- Private and Commercial Pilot Airman Certification Standards (short-field takeoff and maximum performance climb)
