### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can fly a steep, stabilized approach over an obstacle and land at minimum controllable speed on a specified point, stopping in the shortest distance.

### 2. Content

- Prerequisite lessons: [normal approach and landing]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/vii-b-normal-approach-and-landing/' | relative_url }}), [crosswind landing]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-crosswind-landing/' | relative_url }})
- Purpose of the short-field approach and landing
- Real short runways (St. Barts) and an overrun video
- Approach: wider pattern, longer final, steeper descent over the obstacle, corrections when high or low
- Round-out and flare with slight power, no throttle chop, touchdown near power-off stall speed
- Touchdown and roll-out: flaps up, back pressure and maximum braking (PA-28-161 POH)
- Private and Commercial ACS standards; aiming point vs specified touchdown point
- AFH common errors and how to prevent them

### 3. Schedule (ground about 40 min; flight about 45 min)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose and examples | 10 min |
| Approach, round-out and touchdown technique | 15 min |
| Standards, aiming point, common errors | 15 min |
| Flight: demonstration and practice | 45 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck (video)
- PA-28-161 POH (Section 4 short field landing, Section 5 landing chart) and school checklist
- N79NA; runway diagram showing the aiming point and specified touchdown point

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Explain the steeper approach and why approach speed must be held to the round-out.
- Compute landing distance for the day's conditions.
- Demonstrate with callouts, then coach practice landings, choosing a touchdown point.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Compute landing distance and choose the touchdown point.
- Fly a stabilized steep approach at the correct speed.
- Touch down at minimum speed near the point and stop with flaps up, back pressure and maximum braking.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student flies the approach at the recommended speed (+10/−5 kt private, ±5 kt commercial) and touches down within 200 ft beyond the point (private) or 100 ft (commercial).
- The student stops in the shortest practical distance while keeping directional control.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 9 Approaches and Landings (short-field approach and landing, common errors)
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 4 Normal Procedures (short field landing); Section 5 Performance (landing distance)
- Private and Commercial Pilot Airman Certification Standards (short-field approach and landing)
