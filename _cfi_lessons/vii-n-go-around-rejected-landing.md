### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can decide early to go around and perform the go-around correctly in the PA-28-161 (power, attitude, configuration, communication).

### 2. Content

- When to go around (ATC, runway hazards, overtaking, wind shear, wake turbulence, mechanical issues, unstabilized approach, LAHSO)
- Stabilized approach criteria and gates
- When the landing goes wrong: on final, in the round-out, after touchdown; ballooning and bouncing (AFH figures)
- Videos of landing decision points
- Why the decision gets delayed (landing expectancy, pride)
- Cram, climb, clean, call; pitch-up and left-yaw tendency, forward pressure and right rudder
- Flap retraction after a positive rate; AFH go-around sequence figure
- Why the airplane may not climb (premature climb, ground effect, high density altitude)
- Common errors and fixes; summary

### 3. Schedule (ground about 40 min; flight about 40 min)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| When to go around, stabilized approach | 10 min |
| Ballooning, bouncing, decision delay | 10 min |
| Go-around procedure and aircraft handling | 15 min |
| Common errors and review | 5 min |
| Flight: go-arounds from different points on final | 40 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck (videos)
- PA-28-161 POH (Section 4 balked landing) and school checklist
- N79NA

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Treat the go-around as a normal maneuver and agree on go-around criteria before flight.
- Explain the pitch-up and left-yaw tendency when full power is applied with nose-up trim.
- Call for go-arounds at different points, including from the round-out.

### 6. Student's Actions

- State when to go around and the stabilized approach criteria.
- Perform the go-around flow promptly with correct pitch, rudder and flap retraction.
- Make the radio call after the airplane is under control.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student makes a timely go-around decision and applies full power, establishes Vx or Vy, and retracts flaps per the POH after a positive rate of climb.
- The student maintains directional control and avoids obstacles and other traffic.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 9 Approaches and Landings (go-arounds/rejected landings, stabilized approach, ballooning, bouncing)
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 4 Normal Procedures (balked landing)
- Private and Commercial Pilot Airman Certification Standards (go-around/rejected landing)
