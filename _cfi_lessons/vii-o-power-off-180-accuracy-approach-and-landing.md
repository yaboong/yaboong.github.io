### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can glide from abeam a selected point on downwind with the throttle at idle and land on or within 200 ft beyond the point, using judgment of glide angle and energy.

### 2. Content

- What, from where and why
- Judging glide angle and height
- Best glide speed (73 KIAS) and why pitch is the only control with power at idle
- AFH 180° power-off approach path; key positions (downwind key, base key)
- Procedure: touchdown point selection, downwind, closing the throttle abeam, base and final with flaps, final speed 63 KIAS
- Planning the base leg for the wind (AFH figure)
- Corrections when too high or too low; why the glide cannot be stretched
- Common errors and fixes
- Risks: [LAHSO]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-lahso/' | relative_url }}), distraction, collision hazards, [wake turbulence]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-wake-turbulence/' | relative_url }}), [wind shear]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-wind-shear/' | relative_url }})

### 3. Schedule (ground about 40 min; flight about 45 min)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose, glide judgment, best glide speed | 10 min |
| Path, key positions and procedure | 15 min |
| Wind planning, high/low corrections, common errors | 15 min |
| Flight: demonstration and practice | 45 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck
- PA-28-161 POH (best glide speed, approach speeds) and school checklist
- PA-28-161; runway diagram with the selected touchdown point

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Explain the energy picture with power at idle and why pitch controls glide distance only up to best glide speed.
- Show the key positions and wind planning on the whiteboard.
- Demonstrate, then coach the student's judgment at each key position.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Select the touchdown point and close the throttle abeam it.
- Fly best glide, judge the base turn for the wind, and use flaps and slips to manage energy.
- Land on or just beyond the point, or go around if the approach is not safe.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student touches down at or within 200 ft beyond the specified point with the throttle closed from abeam the point, without stretching the glide or forcing the landing.
- The student maintains coordinated flight and safe airspeeds throughout.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 9 Approaches and Landings (power-off accuracy approaches, 180° power-off approach, common errors)
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 3 (best glide speed), Section 4 (approach speeds)
- Commercial Pilot Airman Certification Standards (power-off 180° accuracy approach and landing)
