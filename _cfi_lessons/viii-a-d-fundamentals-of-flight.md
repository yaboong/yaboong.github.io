### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can fly straight-and-level, level turns, climbs, descents and climbing/descending turns in the PA-28-161 by attitude, using outside references first and instruments as a cross-check.
- The student understands how pitch, power and trim work together and recognizes the common errors for each maneuver.

### 2. Content

- The four fundamentals; controls and axes (AFH figures); integrated flight instruction (mostly outside, brief instrument cross-check)
- Pitch and power together set airspeed and flight path; trim (pitch, power, trim; trim holds, does not set attitude); PA-28-161 throttle and trim wheel
- Division of attention and catching small deviations early
- Straight-and-level: nose and wingtip references, cross-check, common errors
- Level turns: rolling in and out, turn classes (shallow, medium, steep), lift components, adverse yaw, [slip vs skid]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-slip-and-skid/' | relative_url }}), common errors
- Climbs and climbing turns: entry, right rudder, Vy 79 / Vx 63 KIAS, common errors
- Descents and descending turns: power, pitch, trim; types of descent; common errors
- Takeaways: eyes outside, pitch first for level/climb, power first for descent, ball centered

### 3. Schedule (ground about 1 hour; flight about 1 hour)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Foundation: controls, attitude flying, pitch/power/trim | 15 min |
| Straight-and-level and level turns | 20 min |
| Climbs, descents and climbing/descending turns | 15 min |
| Common errors and takeaways | 10 min |
| Flight: demonstration and practice of each maneuver | 60 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck
- PA-28-161 POH (Vx, Vy, cruise climb) and school checklist
- PA-28-161 (G5 PFD/HSI); model airplane

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Explain attitude flying with the nose and wingtip references, then show them in the airplane.
- Demonstrate each maneuver with the pitch, power, trim sequence.
- Coach the student's scan: mostly outside, brief instrument cross-check; correct small deviations early.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Explain how pitch, power and trim combine to control airspeed and flight path.
- Fly each maneuver by outside reference, trimming off control pressures.
- Hold altitude, heading, bank and airspeed within standards while scanning for traffic.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student flies each fundamental maneuver smoothly and coordinated, holding altitude ±100 ft, heading ±10°, airspeed ±10 kt and the target bank.
- The student uses trim correctly and maintains a proper outside scan throughout.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 3 Basic Flight Maneuvers (integrated flight instruction, straight-and-level, turns, climbs, descents, common errors)
- Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25), Ch. 5 Aerodynamics of Flight (forces in turns, adverse yaw, slips and skids)
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 4 (climb speeds), Section 7 (flight controls, trim)
- Private Pilot Airman Certification Standards (straight-and-level flight, turns, climbs, descents as covered by basic flight maneuvers)
