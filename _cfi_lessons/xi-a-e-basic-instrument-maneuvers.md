### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can control the PA-28-161 by reference to instruments alone (G5) through straight-and-level flight, constant airspeed climbs and descents, turns to headings and recovery from unusual attitudes, and can handle inadvertent flight into IMC.

### 2. Content

- Foundation: control, performance and navigation instruments; establish–trim–cross-check–adjust; scan patterns and scan errors (fixation, omission, emphasis); six-pack vs G5 instrument errors; primary and supporting instruments per maneuver
- Straight-and-level: pitch, bank, power and trim corrections; IFH figure; G5 panel; common errors
- Constant airspeed climbs and descents: entry, level-off leads; IFH figure; G5 panels; common errors
- Turns to headings: standard rate, bank estimate, rollout lead, turn coordinator and trend vector; IFH figure; common errors
- Unusual attitudes: causes, recognition, nose-high and nose-low recovery with PA-28-161 airspeed limits; G5 panels
- Inadvertent VFR into IMC: recognize, control, get help (bank and descent limits, PAN-PAN/MAYDAY, 121.5, 7700)
- Risks: [personal minimums]({{ '/cfi-lesson-plan/module-personal-minimums/' | relative_url }}), collision hazards, distraction, illusions

### 3. Schedule (ground about 1 hour 15 min; flight about 1 hour with view-limiting device)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Instruments, scan, instrument errors | 20 min |
| Straight-and-level, climbs and descents | 20 min |
| Turns to headings | 10 min |
| Unusual attitudes and inadvertent IMC | 20 min |
| Risks and review | 5 min |
| Flight: each maneuver by reference to instruments | 60 min |
| Postflight debrief | 10 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck
- Instrument Flying Handbook figures; PA-28-161 POH (airspeed limitations)
- PA-28-161 (G5 PFD/HSI); view-limiting device; safety pilot/instructor

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Teach the primary/supporting instrument concept and a radial scan using the G5.
- Demonstrate each maneuver under the view-limiting device; correct scan errors.
- Put the airplane in unusual attitudes and have the student recover; brief the inadvertent IMC procedure.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Identify the primary instruments for each maneuver and use a proper scan.
- Fly each maneuver within standards by reference to instruments only.
- Recognize and recover from nose-high and nose-low unusual attitudes in the correct order.
- State the inadvertent IMC procedure and how to get help.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student holds altitude ±200 ft, heading ±20°, airspeed ±10 kt (private standards) during straight-and-level, climbs, descents and turns to headings.
- The student recognizes unusual attitudes and recovers promptly using the correct sequence without exceeding airplane limits.

### 8. References

- Instrument Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-15), Ch. 5 Flight Instruments; Ch. 6 Airplane Attitude Instrument Flying (using an electronic flight display); Ch. 7 Airplane Basic Flight Maneuvers (straight-and-level, climbs, descents, turns, unusual attitudes)
- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 5 (upset prevention and recovery)
- Aeronautical Information Manual: Ch. 6 (distress and urgency procedures)
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 2 (airspeed limitations); Garmin G5 Pilot's Guide
- Private Pilot Airman Certification Standards (basic instrument maneuvers)
