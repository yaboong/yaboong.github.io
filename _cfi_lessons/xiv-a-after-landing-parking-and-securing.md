### 1. Lesson Objective

- The student can complete the after-landing, parking, engine shutdown, postflight inspection and securing procedures in the PA-28-161 safely and in order.

### 2. Content

- After landing: slow to taxi speed, clear the runway beyond the hold line, after-landing checklist
- Parking: clear of other aircraft and blast, into the wind, nosewheel straight; ramp hand signals (AFH)
- Engine shutdown (school checklist) and passenger control on the ramp
- Postflight walk-around; recording discrepancies (repair, defer under 91.213(d), or cancel)
- Servicing and securing: oil (POH 8.19), fuel, tie-downs, control lock, covers, hangar
- Common errors

### 3. Schedule (ground about 30 min; practiced after each flight)

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| After landing and parking, hand signals | 10 min |
| Shutdown, passengers, postflight inspection | 10 min |
| Servicing, securing, common errors | 10 min |
| Practice after the flight | 15 min |

### 4. Equipment

- Laptop or projector with the deck
- PA-28-161 POH (Section 4 shutdown, Section 8 servicing) and school checklist
- PA-28-161, tie-downs, control lock, covers

### 5. Instructor's Actions

- Brief the sequence and the hand signals.
- After a flight, have the student lead the whole sequence; point out missed items.

### 6. Student's Actions

- Complete the after-landing checklist only after clearing the runway.
- Park, shut down and secure the airplane by checklist, controlling passengers on the ramp.
- Do a postflight walk-around and record any discrepancy correctly.

### 7. Completion Standards

- The student completes all after-landing, shutdown, postflight and securing items in order, without hazard to people or equipment, and records discrepancies correctly.

### 8. References

- Airplane Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-3), Ch. 2 Ground Operations (after landing, parking, engine shutdown, postflight, securing and servicing, hand signals)
- PA-28-161 Pilot's Operating Handbook: Section 4 (stopping engine), Section 8 (servicing, tie-down)
- 14 CFR 91.213(d) (inoperative instruments and equipment)
- Private Pilot Airman Certification Standards (after landing, parking and securing)
